      How it works

      From bid lines to a reviewed EPD pack

      The EPD belongs to the product and is made by the manufacturer. The installer's work is per bid: match, check, calculate and package. EPDesk does that work and keeps a company library that improves with every bid.

      Seven steps from quote to pack

      1Quote readerExcel or CSV in; lines out with code, text, quantity and unit.
      2Product matchE-number, NOBB and GTIN first. Description matching as a fallback, always confirmed by a person.
      3EPD libraryEPDs linked to products, kept in sync with the registries.
      4Clause readerTender text turned into rules: type, standard, verification, validity, GWP.
      5Compliance and CO₂eFixed rules, so every result can be audited. Unit conversion and totals.
      6Pack builderEPD files in line order, the bid, and a report with gaps and supplier requests.
      7Review and learnA named reviewer signs off; confirmed matches feed the company library.
      Uses AI to read and interpretFixed rules and data

      Where an EPD belongs

      LayerWhat it holdsChanges
      ProductThe manufacturer's EPD: type, standard, operator, validity, declared unit, GWP by moduleEvery 5 years or when the product changes
      CompanyYour usual products, pre-matched to their EPDsGrows with each bid
      BidLines × EPDs × client rules → statuses, CO₂e total, packEvery quote

      How we handle your files

      • In this prototype, your bid file is read in your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
      • In a pilot, we agree with you where files are kept, who can see them and when they are deleted.
      • EPDesk sends nothing to clients or suppliers. You download the pack and send it yourself.

      Rules we work by

      • A line is Ready only with an exact code match or a match a person has confirmed, and only when every requirement passes.
      • Every line shows its source, EPD version and the reason for its status.
      • AI reads the bid and the clause; fixed rules decide the status.
      • A named reviewer signs off before a pack is final.
